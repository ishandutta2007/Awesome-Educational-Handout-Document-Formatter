## 🎯 Overview & Scope 📚

Welcome to the definitive curated directory of **Educational Handout & Document Formatter** tools, platforms, and open-source engines! 🎓📝

Whether you are a teacher building interactive worksheets, a professor typesetting rigorous STEM exam papers, an instructional designer formatting study guides, or a developer automating lecture note publishing pipelines, this guide gathers both leading **SaaS visual design suites** and high-performance **open-source typesetting frameworks**.

### 🌟 Key Highlights & Use Cases
- 📐 **STEM & Formula Typesetting:** Fast compilation of equations, diagrams, and problem sets with Typst and LaTeX.
- 🎨 **Visual Worksheets & Infographics:** Drag-and-drop design canvases, brand kits, and multi-page templates.
- 🔄 **Markdown-to-PDF Publishing:** Scriptable document conversion engines using Pandoc, Quarto, and WeasyPrint.
- 🤝 **Collaborative Knowledge Workspaces:** Self-hosted, privacy-first local document editors like AppFlowy and AFFiNE.

## 📊 Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The global digital document creation, desktop publishing, and visual communication software market is valued at approximately **$18B–$22B+ in 2026** (within the broader **$213B–$215B global EdTech ecosystem**), expanding at a ~10%–14% CAGR driven by AI-assisted layout generation and interactive classroom materials. The sector exhibits **strong winner-take-most consolidation** at the top around Canva, Adobe Express, and Google Docs—which together capture the majority of K–12 and higher-ed usage—while niche infographic tools remain **moderately fragmented**, and programmatic STEM/academic handout formatting is dominated by open-source engines like LaTeX and Typst.
